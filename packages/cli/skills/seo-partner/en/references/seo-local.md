# Reference Guide: Local SEO & Google Business Profile

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) & Map Pack

- **Primary category**: Select the most specific category matching the core business offering.
- **NAP Consistency (Name, Address, Phone)**: Identical formatting across GBP, site footer/contact page, `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD, and local citations.
- **Reviews**: Steady acquisition of genuine customer reviews and prompt responses within 48 hours. Zero fake or incentivized reviews.

## 2. Legitimate Local Landing Pages

- Only publish city/area landing pages where the business has real service delivery, case studies, or physical presence (never mass-produce doorway pages by swapping city names).
