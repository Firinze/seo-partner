## 2. Confirmed Core Rules

| Area | Confirmed Rule | Official Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indexing | A `noindex` directive (meta tag or X-Robots-Tag header) prevents indexing completely, even if the URL is listed in `sitemap.xml`. | Google Search Central — Block Search indexing |
| Canonicals | `rel="canonical"` is a strong hint and must point to an indexable HTTP 200 URL consistent with internal links and sitemaps. | Google Search Central — Consolidate duplicate URLs |
| Titles & Snippets | Google has no hard character count limit for ranking, but SERP display typically truncates titles around 50–60 characters (~600px) and descriptions around 150–160 characters. | Google Search Central — Title links & snippets |
| AI & Content | Scaled content abuse (generating many pages without original value for users) violates Google spam policies regardless of whether AI or humans produced it. | Google Spam Policies — Scaled content abuse |
| Structured Data | JSON-LD markup must accurately represent content visible to users on the page. | General Structured Data Guidelines |

## 3. Epistemic Discipline

Any unconfirmed ranking rumor or third-party study MUST be explicitly labeled **"Reported, to be verified"**.
