# Reference Guide: Keyword Strategy & Mapping

## 1. Search Intent Classification

1. **Transactional / High-Commercial**: pricing, quote, comparison, service hire (`pricing`, `agency`, `software`, `best`). Top priority for revenue impact.
2. **Local**: service + city/area or `near me`. Targets Google Business Profile and local landing pages.
3. **Informational**: how-to, templates, problem diagnosis. Builds topical authority and internal link equity to money pages.
4. **Navigational**: brand or product name queries.

## 2. Golden Rule: 1 Primary Query = 1 Canonical Page

- Every indexable public URL targets **one primary search intent** and its closely related secondary variations.
- **Cannibalization resolution**: when two pages compete for the same query:
  - **Merge** (301 redirect the weaker URL into the stronger one), or
  - **Differentiate** intent (e.g., transactional service page vs educational guide), or
  - **Canonicalize** if both URLs must exist for UX reasons.
