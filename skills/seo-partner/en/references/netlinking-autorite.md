# Reference Guide: Link Building & Domain Authority

## 1. Sustainable Acquisition Principles

- Prioritize topical relevance and genuine editorial authority over raw backlink volume.
- **Zero PBNs or paid link schemes**: any paid placement must carry `rel="sponsored"` or `rel="nofollow"`.

## 2. Core Authority Levers

1. **Linkable Assets**: calculators, open tools, original industry benchmarks, and data studies.
2. **Digital PR & Ecosystem Partnerships**: joint case studies, partner integrations, expert commentary.
3. **Unlinked Brand Mentions**: reclaim existing mentions of the brand or founders into contextual links.
