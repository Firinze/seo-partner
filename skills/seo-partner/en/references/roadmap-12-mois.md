# Reference Guide: 12-Month SEO Roadmap

## Quarter 1 (M1–M3): Technical Foundations & Quick Wins

- Resolve all indexing blockers (`robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, accidental `noindex`, canonicals).
- Fix unique `title`, `description`, single `H1`, and `JSON-LD` schemas across existing pages.
- Optimize striking-distance pages ranking in positions 4 to 15.

## Quarter 2 (M4–M6): Pillar/Cluster Architecture & Local SEO

- Launch core commercial pillar pages and structured internal linking.
- Optimize Google Business Profile, NAP citations, and legitimate local service pages.

## Quarter 3 (M7–M9): Content Expansion, GEO & Authority

- Publish cluster guides targeting informational and generative search queries.
- Execute digital PR and linkable asset campaigns.

## Quarter 4 (M10–M12): Content Refresh, Conversion Optimization & Expansion

- Refresh Q1/Q2 pages based on Search Console query data, prune cannibalization, and expand to new regions/languages.
