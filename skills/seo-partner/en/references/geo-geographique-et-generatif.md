# Reference Guide: Geographic Targeting & Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

## 1. International & Multilingual SEO (`hreflang`)

- Every language/region variant (`/en/`, `/fr/`, `/en-gb/`) must include reciprocal `hreflang` annotations including self-reference and `x-default`.
- Never auto-redirect search crawlers based on geolocation IP.

## 2. Generative Engine Optimization (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity)

- Structure content with direct, factual, citation-ready answers immediately following question-based `H2` headings.
- Publish original benchmarks, clean comparison tables, and verifiable data points.
- Reinforce brand entity clarity (`Organization` JSON-LD, consistent authoritative third-party mentions).
